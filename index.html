Their Story

Two hearts, one thread

Every good love story begins small, and ours began over filter coffee at a friend's engagement in Bengaluru — one borrowed umbrella, a sudden March downpour, and a conversation that outlasted the rain.

Five years, two cities and a thousand shared plates of dosa later, we are writing our next chapter in the oldest language we know — love, spoken this time in jasmine, turmeric and gold. Nothing would mean more than to have you beside us when we do.
